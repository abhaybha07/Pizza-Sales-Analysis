# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis | MySQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data to identify revenue trends, customer ordering patterns, and the best- and worst-performing pizzas.

The project involves data preparation, SQL-based analysis, and the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard to present business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- *MySQL:* Data cleaning, querying, and analysis
- *Power BI:* Interactive dashboard development and visualization
- *DAX:* Calculated measures and business KPIs
- *Power Query:* Data transformation and date formatting

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains two pages:

*1. Sales Overview*

Provides an overview of revenue, order trends, pizza categories, pizza sizes, and key business metrics.

*2. Best & Worst Sellers Analysis*

Compares the top five and bottom five pizzas based on revenue, quantity sold, and order frequency.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Friday recorded the highest order volume.
- July and January were among the strongest months for orders.
- The Classic category contributed the highest share of revenue.
- Large pizzas accounted for the highest share of pizza sales.
- The Thai Chicken Pizza was among the highest revenue-generating products.
- The Brie Carre Pizza was among the lowest-performing products by sales volume.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Plan inventory and staffing around higher-demand periods, particularly Fridays.
- Investigate the factors behind stronger sales in January and July.
- Prioritize availability of popular pizza categories and sizes.
- Review low-performing products before making menu or promotional decisions.
